# Acceptable Use Policy

**Unlimited Vibes, Inc.**
**Last Updated: August 7, 2026**

---

## Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the boundaries of permitted use for the Unlimited Vibes platform. It protects our users, our systems, and the integrity of compliance data.

---

## Prohibited Uses

You may NOT use Unlimited Vibes to:

### 1. Circumvent Compliance
- Game your compliance score through false data
- Use scan results to craft content that technically passes but is intentionally misleading
- Submit content for scanning that you know violates regulations, with intent to find loopholes
- Misrepresent your compliance score to brands or partners

### 2. Illegal Activity
- Facilitate fraud, money laundering, or financial crimes
- Distribute content that exploits minors
- Violate sanctions, export controls, or trade restrictions
- Stalk, harass, or threaten others

### 3. System Abuse
- Exceed published API rate limits
- Attempt to access other users' data
- Reverse-engineer our AI models or scoring algorithms
- Inject malicious content to manipulate AI responses
- Perform load testing without written permission
- Automate account creation

### 4. Misrepresentation
- Impersonate another person or organization
- Falsely claim Unlimited Vibes endorsement or certification
- Use our brand assets without permission
- Represent AI-generated compliance reports as human legal advice

### 5. Resale Without Authorization
- Resell access to the platform without an Agency plan
- White-label our reports without Agency-tier licensing
- Build competing products using our API outputs

## Rate Limits

| Plan | API Calls/min | Scans/month | Concurrent Requests |
|------|--------------|-------------|--------------------|
| Starter | 10 | 50 | 2 |
| Pro | 60 | Unlimited | 10 |
| Agency | 120 | Unlimited | 25 |

Exceeding limits: requests are throttled (429 status), not blocked. Persistent abuse triggers account review.

---

## Content Guidelines

Content submitted for scanning must:
- Be your own or you must have rights to submit it
- Not contain malware, exploits, or injection attacks
- Not be submitted solely to probe our AI for vulnerabilities

We process content for compliance analysis only. We do not store full content beyond 90 days.

---

## Enforcement

| Severity | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Minor first offense | Warning email + 24h cool-down |
| Repeated minor violations | 7-day account suspension |
| Major violation | Immediate termination |
| Illegal activity | Termination + report to authorities |

Appeals: email legal@unlimitedvibes.com within 14 days of action.

---

## Reporting Violations

Report AUP violations to: abuse@unlimitedvibes.com

Include:
- Description of the violation
- Evidence (screenshots, URLs)
- Your contact information

We investigate all reports within 48 hours.

---

## Changes

We may update this AUP with 14 days notice. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
