# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Unlimited Vibes, Inc.**
**Last Updated: August 7, 2026**

---

## 1. Scope

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the Terms of Service between Unlimited Vibes, Inc. ("Processor") and the customer ("Controller") and governs the processing of personal data.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **Personal Data**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person
- **Processing**: Any operation performed on personal data (collection, storage, analysis, deletion)
- **Sub-processor**: Third party engaged by Processor to process Personal Data
- **Data Subject**: The individual whose Personal Data is processed

---

## 3. Processing Details

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Nature of processing | AI-powered content analysis for regulatory compliance |
| Purpose | Compliance scanning, scoring, monitoring |
| Categories of data subjects | Content creators, their mentioned contacts |
| Types of personal data | Names, email, content text, platform usernames, IP addresses |
| Duration | Duration of service agreement + 30 days |
| Jurisdiction | United States |

---

## 4. Processor Obligations

The Processor shall:

- Process data only on documented instructions from Controller
- Ensure persons authorized to process data are under confidentiality obligations
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Not engage Sub-processors without prior written authorization
- Assist Controller with Data Subject rights requests
- Delete or return all Personal Data upon termination
- Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance
- Allow and contribute to audits (with reasonable notice)

---

## 5. Security Measures

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Access controls with role-based permissions
- Regular security assessments and penetration testing
- Incident detection and response procedures
- Employee security training
- Secure development lifecycle (SDLC)

---

## 6. Sub-processors

Current authorized Sub-processors:

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Location | Safeguards |
|---------------|---------|----------|------------|
| Anthropic | AI content analysis | USA | DPA + SCCs |
| Stripe | Payment processing | USA | PCI DSS Level 1 |
| Neon (PostgreSQL) | Database hosting | USA | SOC 2 |
| Railway/Render | Application hosting | USA | SOC 2 |

Controller will be notified 14 days before new Sub-processors are added.

---

## 7. International Transfers

For transfers of EEA/UK Personal Data to the USA:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2: Controller to Processor) apply
- Transfer Impact Assessment available upon request
- Supplementary measures implemented per EDPB guidance

---

## 8. Data Breach Notification

- Processor will notify Controller of a breach within 72 hours of becoming aware
- Notification includes: nature of breach, categories of data affected, estimated number of data subjects, measures taken
- Processor will cooperate with Controller's breach response

---

## 9. Data Subject Rights

Processor will assist Controller in responding to:
- Access requests (within 30 days)
- Deletion requests (within 30 days)
- Portability requests (machine-readable format)
- Rectification requests
- Restriction/objection requests

---

## 10. Term and Termination

This DPA remains in effect for the duration of the service agreement. Upon termination:
- All Personal Data deleted within 30 days
- Certification of deletion provided upon request
- Anonymized aggregate data may be retained for analytics
