# FTC Disclosure Guidelines for Creators

**Unlimited Vibes Creator Compliance Guide**
**Last Updated: August 7, 2026**

This guide explains FTC endorsement rules in plain language. Our compliance engine enforces these automatically, but creators should understand the "why" behind each rule.

---

## The Core Rule

**If there's a material connection between you and a brand, you must disclose it clearly.**

A "material connection" includes:
- Payment (cash, products, services)
- Free products or gifts
- Affiliate relationships (commission on sales)
- Employee/contractor relationships
- Family relationships with the brand

---

## What Counts as Clear Disclosure

### DO

- Use **#ad** or **#sponsored** at the BEGINNING of a caption (not buried)
- Say "Paid partnership with [Brand]" clearly
- Use platform-native disclosure tools (Instagram Paid Partnership tag, YouTube paid promotion checkbox)
- Disclose in EVERY post, story, and video (even reposts)
- Make disclosure visible without clicking "more"

### DON'T

- Bury #ad in a wall of hashtags
- Use ambiguous tags like #sp #collab #partner alone
- Disclose only in a bio or "about" page
- Assume viewers saw a previous disclosure
- Put disclosure where it requires scrolling, clicking, or swiping

---

## Platform-Specific Rules

### Instagram
- Use "Paid Partnership" tag (required for sponsored content)
- #ad in first line of caption (before the fold)
- Stories: text overlay "AD" or "Paid Partnership" visible for full duration
- Reels: spoken disclosure AND text in first 3 seconds

### TikTok
- Toggle "Branded Content" in post settings
- #ad in video description AND spoken/text on screen
- Disclosure must appear before the product pitch
- Duets/Stitches of branded content need their own disclosure

### YouTube
- Check "Includes paid promotion" in video settings
- Verbal disclosure in first 30 seconds
- Don't rely solely on the YouTube automatic label
- Description box disclosure alone is NOT sufficient

### Twitter/X
- #ad at beginning of tweet (not end)
- Thread: disclose in first tweet AND any tweet that promotes the product
- Quote tweets of brand content need separate disclosure

---

## Special Cases

### Affiliate Links
- Must disclose: "I earn a commission if you purchase through this link"
- Short form OK: "Affiliate link" or "#affiliate #ad"
- Amazon Associates: "As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases"

### Free Products / Gifted Items
- Must disclose even if no payment: "Gifted by [Brand]" or "PR package"
- Applies even if you weren't asked to post
- "#gifted" alone is ambiguous; add context

### AI-Generated Content
- Emerging requirement: disclose when content is AI-generated or AI-assisted
- Use: "Created with AI" or platform-native AI labels
- Some platforms (TikTok, YouTube) require AI disclosure in settings

### Health & Supplement Claims
- Cannot claim a product "cures", "treats", or "prevents" any condition unless FDA-approved
- "Results may vary" is NOT sufficient disclaimer
- Personal testimonials still need scientific backing for claims
- Unlimited Vibes flags these automatically

### Financial Claims
- Cannot guarantee income or returns
- Must disclose typical results, not best-case
- "Not financial advice" disclaimer recommended
- FTC + SEC may both apply for investment content

## Enforcement & Penalties

| Violation Level | Typical Outcome |
|----------------|------------------|
| First offense (minor) | Warning letter from FTC |
| Repeated minor violations | Consent order (legally binding) |
| Willful/deceptive violations | $50,000+ per violation |
| Brand + creator liability | Both parties can be fined |
